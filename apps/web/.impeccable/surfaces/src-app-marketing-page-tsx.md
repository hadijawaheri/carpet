# Landing surface brief

Scope: the home route (landing) and, by inheritance, every Farsh surface. Visitor mode: Experience (the carpet leads from the first viewport).

Audience: design reviewers arriving from Hadi's résumé link; secondarily carpet-curious visitors. Action: grab a real carpet and feel silk vs wool vs machine-made; then go deeper into a carpet or the research. Proof: Hadi's own carpet photos as 3D cloth, the cloth presets, real knot-density facts. Constraints: red + cream pinned; never draw carpets; Persian RTL; reduced-motion gets the still photo.

Memorable moment: lifting the carpet's corner like a conservator and seeing its back, knots counted under a loupe.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a carpet museum's study gallery, not a shop. Each carpet hangs as an accessioned specimen under raking light; the visitor handles it. Refuses the category default: cream luxury store with a hero banner over a product grid.

OWN-WORLD: Gallery walls drenched in madder red (the colour carpet museums paint their halls), cream mount-board panels and accession labels as the only light surfaces, indigo for the loupe ring and focus, saffron hairlines as gallery rails. Type: Iranian Naskh serif for museum inscriptions, Vazirmatn for labels, a mono for accession numbers. Components are labels, rails, mounts, a loupe: no rounded cards, no shadows except the carpet's own.

STORY: First the visitor meets one carpet, life-size under light, and touches it. Then they compare materials in the same hand, learn to read knots (raj, shaneh) from the specimen itself, and leave knowing these carpets differ in ways a photo hides.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed madder-red wall. The 3D carpet hangs centre-start at ~70% viewport height, swaying in slow air, casting a real shadow on the wall. Beside it at the end edge a cream accession label: number, name, material, size, density, plus the material switch (silk / wool / machine) as the label's own fields. The museum inscription headline sits large across the wall above the label. Primary action: grab the corner (cursor hint on the carpet), secondary: walk to the next specimen.

FORM: Carpet Museum conservation and study gallery; candidate 5 of 7 in my ordered list; seed key 49713511 (degraded roll, no challengers).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
